Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about NOUN Exam Summary Hub, payments, and course access.

What is NOUN Summaries all about?

NOUN Summaries Hub is a web app developed to help students pass their NOUN exams easily by reading summaries instead of drowning in the whole course material.

How does the Premium payment work?

Premium payments are special payments. Pay for Premium once and you never have to pay again. All courses will be available to you, and you can download them as you like.

How does the payment work?

The payment section is very easy. We use a manual payment method: make a payment for a course, upload your correct payment receipt, and wait for approval. If your payment was successful, it will be approved.

What if I made a payment but mistakenly uploaded another screenshot?

Don't panic. If you uploaded the wrong receipt, our team can help. In your dashboard, there is a WhatsApp button. Click it and explain your problem to customer support. Make sure you actually paid and include your request there, and your problem will be solved.

What if I make a fake payment?

All payments are manually verified and will be rejected if payment is not received.

What if I don't pay the exact amount?

Pay the exact amount shown. Payments are reviewed before approval. Any payment less than the required amount will be rejected, and there will be no refunds.
